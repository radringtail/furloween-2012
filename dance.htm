The Dance

Date 10/27/2012
Time 6pm - 2am
Where Orlando Elk's Lodge
Cost $30

The Furloween Dance Party. For over ten years we've been working, refining and changing things here and there to make this the most fun possible. It started as a Halloween house party that grew out of control so rapidly that it had to move; thus, Furloween was born.

We'll be at the Orlando Elk's Lodge again this year. We love the space and each year the decorations get better and better. The party has a cover charge of $30. This pays for the space, catered food, non-alchoholic beverages, lighting, dance, and generally is the budget we use to throw the best party possible. There's a costume contest and one special game. Video games will also be on-site. There will be a cash bar available. We will be checking IDs and doing the wrist band thing (for those of age). You can mingle, dance, play videogames, whatever you want! The entire purpose of this party is simply to celebrate our friendships and each other.

We'll have some artists, DJ's, and candy. Costumes are welcomed, encouraged, and begged to (please please please) show up! There will be a green room for changing into costume and cooling off. If anyone has some fans they'd like to bring, let us know in your registration form!

As always! This party is for all ages. Anyone under the age of 18 will need a Parental/Guardian Permission Form.
