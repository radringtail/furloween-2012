Code of Conduct

Or: How NOT to Get Thrown Out of the Elk's Lodge

I trust you guys to keep your friends sane. So here's the bit for those of you new to things!

  • Do party things. Treat the Elk's Lodge as you would want your home treated.
  • Treat each other and the Elk's with respect. Listen to the folks who are running the party. Their sole purpose is to see you have a safe, fun time.
  • Don't drink outside the Lodge; don't terrorize the neighborhood (Though really, that was awesome. Buy me a drink and I'll tell you the story.)

We'll have a few folks on hand doing party patrol. If you need help, just grab one of them or someone from registration. One more thing, please don't bring any alcohol of your own. It can cause the Elk's to lose their liquor license. It's not like thier prices are high. If it's really bad, and you GOTTA have a drink... Hell Just ask Mach for one. He'll be like, "Ummm who are you? Oh, okay. "

Last year was fantastic! We didn't have a single problem and I'd love to see this become a regular thing. Thanks, and can't wait to see you folks there!
